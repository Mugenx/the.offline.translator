Watch translation progress

The side panel shows how much of the page is translated while it happens, with a live percentage and a count for every segment.

Segments

offline.translator translates a page in segments: pieces of text such as a heading, a paragraph or a menu item. Progress is counted in segments, so 212 / 496 means 212 of the page's 496 segments are done, shown as 43%.

Reading the panel

Percentage and count
How much of the page is translated, for example 43% and 212 / 496. The bar under it fills as segments finish.
Total segs
All segments found on the page.
Cache hits
Segments loaded from the translation cache on your device instead of being translated again.
New
Segments translated for the first time.
Queued
Segments waiting their turn.
Done
Everything in scope is translated. When the whole page is finished, the panel says “Page translation is up to date across the full document.”

Viewport or All

Viewport is the fast mode. It translates the segments on screen first and picks up new ones as you scroll.

All translates the full page in one pass, including the parts you have not scrolled to yet. You can switch between the two at any time.

The cache

Finished translations are stored in IndexedDB in your browser and reused on return visits. Text that repeats across pages, like navigation menus and footers, is translated once and then loaded from the cache, so it counts as a cache hit instead of a new segment.

Translations refined with Gemini Nano are saved back to the cache too. Clear Cache in the side panel empties it, and Restore puts the page back in its original language.

If progress stops

  • Check that Chrome is version 138 or newer at chrome://version.
  • Open chrome://on-device-translation-internals and check that the language pack for your pair is on this device. The first download for a pair can take a moment.
  • Polishing with Gemini Nano needs the Prompt API. chrome://on-device-internals shows whether it is available on your machine.
